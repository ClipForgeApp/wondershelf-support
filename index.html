WonderShelf Support

WonderShelf is an ad-free children's bookshelf of interactive WonderPages and watercolor painting. We collect no tracking data and keep each child's first name, age, and artwork on the device.

Get Help

For WonderShelf questions, app issues, accessibility feedback, or App Review support, open an issue on GitHub. GitHub issues are public, so a parent or caregiver should post and leave out any personal information.

Delete Account

WonderShelf does not create a server account. To delete the local account and saved art:

  1. Open WonderShelf.
  2. Tap the profile button on the bookshelf.
  3. Choose Delete WonderShelf Account & Local Data.
  4. Confirm deletion. The app removes the first name, age, and saved paintings from the device.

Privacy

WonderShelf has no ads, third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, or external accounts. The app uses Sign in with Apple only to start a private local shelf experience.

Family Use

Books are organized by age range, and earlier shelves remain available when a reader grows into a new age band.
